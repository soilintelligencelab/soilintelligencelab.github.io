Research

Key questions about the soil carbon cycle — how carbon forms, how long it persists, and how organic and inorganic carbon respond to environmental change and land management — remain unresolved. That gap keeps science from informing the policy and industry that need verifiable methods for soil carbon sequestration. We work to close it.

Mechanistic models

Process-based soil and land carbon models, from soil columns to Earth system models.

Big data

Global soil profiles, radiocarbon, field trials and remote sensing, brought together through data assimilation.

AI

Process-guided, interpretable machine learning that learns mechanisms instead of just fitting patterns.
