Feng works at the interface of soil carbon science, mechanistic modelling and AI to understand the global soil carbon cycle and its response to climate change. Before joining NTU as a Nanyang Assistant Professor in 2026, Feng was an Assistant Professor in the Department of Informatics and Intelligent Systems and the Institute of Energy and the Environment (co-hire) at the Pennsylvania State University (2025–2026), and an Eric & Wendy Schmidt AI in Science Postdoctoral Fellow in Ecology & Evolutionary Biology at Cornell University (2023–2025), working with Ben Houlton on AI for scalable enhanced rock weathering. In 2019, Feng was a visiting fellow at the Global Soil Partnership Secretariat of the UN Food and Agriculture Organization in Rome, mapping the global potential for soil organic carbon sequestration.

Feng received a Ph.D. with honors from the Department of Earth System Science, Tsinghua University (2023), advised by Yiqi Luo and Xiaomeng Huang, including a joint Ph.D. period at the Max Planck Institute for Biogeochemistry in Jena (2021–2022) with Markus Reichstein and Marion Schrumpf. Feng holds a B.Sc. with honors in Environmental Science and Engineering from Sun Yat-sen University (2018).
