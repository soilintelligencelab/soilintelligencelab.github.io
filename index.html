We study the soil carbon cycle — the largest carbon pool on land — by fusing mechanistic models, big data and AI, to find verifiable, gigaton-scale pathways for removing carbon dioxide from the atmosphere.

Soils hold more carbon than the atmosphere and vegetation combined, as both organic matter and inorganic minerals. How that carbon forms, how long it persists, and how it responds to a changing climate and to management remain open questions — and they stand between climate science and credible soil-based climate solutions.

The Soil Intelligence Lab, led by Feng Tao at the {{ site.department }}, {{ site.institution }}, builds the process-guided AI tools, global data syntheses and models needed to answer them.
