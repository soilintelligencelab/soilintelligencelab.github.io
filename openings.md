### PhD students

We are looking for students interested in soil biogeochemistry and its responses to climate change. You do not need prior publications or a fully formed thesis idea — a good sense of the research questions that excite you matters most. Current themes include:

- **Soil organic carbon** — global soil carbon age and turnover from radiocarbon data; microbial physiology (e.g., carbon use efficiency) and its environmental controls
- **Enhanced rock weathering** — key limitations on CO₂ removal; the long-term fate of captured carbon across soil–river–ocean systems; organic–inorganic carbon interactions
- **Cross-scale carbon cycle models** — the COUSINE model (COUpled Soil Inorganic-organic carbon model for eNhanced wEathering)
- **Process-guided AI** — Biogeochemistry-Informed Neural Networks (BINN) and foundation models for biogeochemistry

PhD positions come with a full scholarship. The earliest start is the January 2027 intake. Getting in touch early is strongly encouraged.
